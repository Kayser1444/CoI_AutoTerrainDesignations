# Navigate fixed terrain work as projected ground

V2 will overlay existing terrain-designation targets onto captured terrain and
navigate the resulting surface with one clearance-exact Mega ground graph.
Propagating generated accessways remain limited to V flat and ramp profiles.
One bounded transition adapter at a projected-ground boundary may generate
V-prime corner profiles. A jagged fringe uses one longitudinal slice; a slanted
fringe may use one complementary two-slice pair, but never a third slice. This
allows both fringes to join an ordinary V route or another projected surface
without admitting chained V-prime search. Existing corner or other physically
traversable shapes remain navigable without generation. This replaces exposed
fixed frontages and optimistic provider terminals, allows exact reuse across
origin clusters, and keeps generated ownership authoritative at every boundary.
